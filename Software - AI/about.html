About, sources & editorial policy

Saiful the Techie is turning two course curricula into a structured hands-on learning hub. The curricula remain the roadmap backbone; explanations, labs, projects and curated resources are our tutorial implementation.

SOURCE CURRICULUM

What comes from the uploaded curricula

Module names, milestone sequence and listed lesson topics. We preserve these as the source map.

TUTORIAL EXPANSION

What we add

Explanations, examples, labs, debugging guidance, quizzes, projects, resource curation and learning flow.

ST RECOMMENDED

Audit additions

Important professional topics that deserve explicit attention even where the syllabus gives them limited coverage, such as accessibility, responsive stress testing and browser DevTools.
