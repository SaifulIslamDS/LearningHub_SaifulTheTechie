## Why the roadmap changed in v0.5.2

The learning-path audit found that the prior sequence jumped from Level 01 Module 52 directly into Level 02, leaving **Level 01 Modules 53–67** unauthored. It also did not explicitly schedule the Level 02 Bonus professional modules.

For a coherent beginner → advanced → career-ready path, Level 01 must be completed before Level 02 expansion, and the professional masterclass/career layer must exist before v1.0.
