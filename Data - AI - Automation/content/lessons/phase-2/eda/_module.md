**Exploratory data analysis (EDA)** is how you get to know a dataset before you report on it or model it: what's in it, what's normal, what's strange, and which questions it can actually answer. Good EDA is driven by **questions**, uses simple summaries and charts, and ends with **written findings**, not a pile of plots.

The charts in these lessons are made with matplotlib and the small `chart_style.py` helper in [data-science-scripts.zip](../../../downloads/phase-2/data-science-scripts.zip) (colour-blind-checked colours and quiet axes). The Visualization module explains how to build them yourself.

**By the end of this module you can:** turn a vague request into analytical questions, describe distributions and patterns over time and across groups, measure relationships between variables, and separate genuine anomalies from data errors.
