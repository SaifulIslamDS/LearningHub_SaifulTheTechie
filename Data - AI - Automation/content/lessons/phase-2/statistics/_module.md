**Statistics** turns "the numbers look different" into "the difference is (or isn't) bigger than chance". You need a small, solid core: how to describe the centre and spread of data, which common distributions describe counts and yes/no outcomes, how to measure relationships, and how to state **uncertainty** with a confidence interval. Every later topic, from A/B tests to machine learning evaluation, builds on these.

The scripts use **SciPy** (`scipy.stats`) alongside pandas: `11_stats_center_spread.py` and `12_stats_correlation_ci.py` in [data-science-scripts.zip](../../../downloads/phase-2/data-science-scripts.zip).

**By the end of this module you can:** choose mean, median or trimmed mean; measure spread with IQR, standard deviation and CV; recognise normal, log-normal, Poisson and binomial data; compute and explain correlations; and report a mean, a proportion or a median with a 95% confidence interval.
