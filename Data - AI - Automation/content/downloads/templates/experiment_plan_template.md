# Experiment plan — <short name>

> Fill this in and get it agreed **before** any data is collected. Changing it afterwards must be written down with a reason.

| Field | Your answer |
|---|---|
| **Decision this informs** | What will we do differently depending on the result? |
| **Research question** | One sentence, specific and measurable |
| **Hypothesis** | "Doing X will change Y by about Z, because …" |
| **Population / eligibility** | Who or what is included and excluded |
| **Unit of randomisation** | Order, session, customer, store … (and why) |
| **Treatment / control** | Exactly what each group gets |
| **Primary metric** | One metric, with its exact definition |
| **Guardrail metrics** | What must not get worse |
| **Minimum detectable effect** | Smallest effect worth acting on, and why |
| **Sample size & duration** | From a power analysis (α, power, baseline) |
| **Analysis plan** | Test, confidence interval, segments planned in advance |
| **Stopping rule** | Fixed end date, or a sequential method |
| **Risks & pitfalls** | Contamination, novelty, seasonality, sample ratio mismatch … |
| **Owner & dates** | Who runs it, start, end, readout |
