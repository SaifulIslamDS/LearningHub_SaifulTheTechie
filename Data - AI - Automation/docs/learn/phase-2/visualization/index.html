Phase 2 · Data Science

Visualization

Move from BI reporting into analytical programming and model thinking.

Charts are how most people will meet your analysis. This module covers the three Python libraries you’ll use most — matplotlib (full control, the base of the others), seaborn (statistical charts from a DataFrame in one line) and Plotly (interactive charts for exploration and the web) — and then the part that matters most: turning a chart into a message.

The scripts are 15_viz_matplotlib_seaborn.py and 16_viz_plotly_storytelling.py in data-science-scripts.zip. They share chart_style.py: a colour-blind-checked palette, quiet axes and a save() helper. Copy it into your own projects.

By the end of this module you can: build line, bar, small-multiple, box and heatmap charts; make an interactive chart to share; and redesign a busy chart so that a manager gets the point in five seconds.
